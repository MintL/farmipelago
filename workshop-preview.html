Three ways to make a workshop.

01 / Timberwright

Honey timber, a deep oxblood gable, loft shutters and a sheltered tool porch. A warm, well-used farm repair shop.

02 / Fieldworks

Teal steel, twin sawtooth skylights and an exposed lifting gantry. A compact, purposeful machinery workshop.

03 / Copperhearth

Stone piers, a stepped copper roof and a tall forge chimney. A little island foundry with a working service yard.
